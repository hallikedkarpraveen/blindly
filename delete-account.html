You are in complete control of your Blindly account and personal information. You can permanently delete your account at any time directly inside the Blindly mobile app, or submit a deletion request on the web below without reinstalling the app.

Option 1 · In-App (Instant) iOS & Android

Delete Directly in the Blindly App

If you have the Blindly app installed, you can delete your account immediately in four steps:

  1. Open the Blindly app on your iOS or Android device and sign in.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar, then tap Settings (gear icon).
  3. Select Account → Delete Account.
  4. Confirm your choice by tapping Permanently Delete Account.
Note: Uninstalling the Blindly app from your phone does not automatically delete your account. Follow the steps above or use Option 2 to permanently erase your data.
Option 2 · Web Request (No App Required) Processed within 7 days

Delete via Web Form or Email

If you have already uninstalled the app or cannot log in, you can request full account deletion or partial data deletion right from this page:

  • Submit the Web Deletion Request Form below using your registered phone number or email address.
  • Or email info@blindly.date directly with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and the phone number or email linked to your Blindly account.

Data Retention & Erasure Policy

What happens when you delete your Blindly account

Permanently Deleted Data

When your account deletion is confirmed, the following data is permanently deleted from our active databases immediately (or within 7 days for verified web/email requests):

  • Account Identifiers: Phone number, email address, display name, and authentication tokens.
  • Profile & Preferences: Bio, age, gender, mood preferences, and profile prompts.
  • Voice & Audio Data: All voice introductions, voice prompts, and in-chat voice notes.
  • Photos & Media: Uploaded profile photos and verification selfies.
  • Social & Activity Data: Matches, blind chat threads, messages, and approximate location history.
Limited Retention & Timeframes

Certain limited records may be temporarily retained strictly for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance before automatic purge:

  • Encrypted System Backups: Residual copies in encrypted disaster-recovery backups are automatically overwritten within 30 days.
  • Safety, Ban & Abuse Logs: If an account was reported or suspended for violating our Community Guidelines or Child Safety Standards, minimal hashed identifiers and incident logs are retained for up to 180 days to prevent repeat abuse and comply with the Indian Information Technology Rules, 2021.
  • Financial / Legal Records: Where required by applicable tax or statutory law, transaction metadata (excluding profile, voice, or chat content) is retained only for the legally mandated period.

For more details on how we handle personal data, read our Privacy Policy or contact our support and grievance team on our Support Center.
