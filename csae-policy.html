Child Safety Standards

Our zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation.

Legal Privacy Policy Terms of Use Child Safety Standards Safety & Guidelines
Last updated: August 16, 2026

Blindly, operated by AYRAVT, is committed to providing a safe and respectful environment for all users. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy toward Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).

Protecting children is a fundamental responsibility, and we are committed to preventing the misuse of our platform for any activity involving the exploitation or abuse of minors.

1. Adults-Only Platform

Blindly is intended exclusively for adults aged 18 years and older.

Individuals under the age of 18 are strictly prohibited from:

  • Creating an account
  • Accessing the platform
  • Using any Blindly features
  • Communicating with other users

Any account identified as belonging to a minor will be permanently suspended and removed from the platform.

2. Zero-Tolerance Policy

Blindly strictly prohibits any content or behavior involving:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)
  • Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)
  • Grooming or solicitation of minors
  • Sexualization or exploitation of minors
  • Any attempt to exploit, endanger, or abuse a child
  • Any content that promotes, facilitates, or depicts child abuse

Any violation of this policy will result in immediate enforcement action.

3. Safety Measures

Blindly employs multiple safety measures to help protect our community, including:

  • Age eligibility requirements during registration
  • User reporting tools available throughout the application
  • Automated systems to help identify suspicious content or activity
  • Human review of reported accounts and content
  • Prompt investigation of reported violations
  • Permanent removal of accounts that violate our Child Safety Standards

Our safety processes are continuously reviewed and improved to help maintain a secure environment for our users.

4. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

If you encounter or suspect any content, account, message, or behavior involving child exploitation or abuse, please report it immediately.

Report within the App

You can report directly from:

  • User Profiles
  • Chat Messages
  • Shared Media
  • Other user-generated content

Tap Report and select the appropriate reason.

Email Reporting

You may also contact our Safety Team directly:

Urgent safety email: safety@blindly.date

Child safety reports receive the highest priority for review.

5. Enforcement Actions

When Blindly identifies content or behavior involving CSAE or CSAM, we may take one or more of the following actions:

  • Immediate permanent account suspension
  • Removal of illegal or prohibited content
  • Restriction or termination of associated accounts where appropriate
  • Preservation of relevant information where required by applicable law
  • Cooperation with law enforcement authorities as legally required

6. Reporting to Appropriate Authorities

Where required by applicable law, Blindly reports identified Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and related child exploitation activity to the appropriate authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) for applicable U.S. cases, or other relevant law enforcement agencies.

Blindly fully cooperates with lawful investigations relating to child exploitation and abuse.

7. Contact Information & Support

For child safety concerns or urgent reports:

Blindly Safety Team: safety@blindly.date

General Support: support@blindly.date

Our Commitment

Blindly is committed to maintaining a safe, respectful, and trusted platform for adults. We continuously evaluate and improve our safety policies, reporting mechanisms, and moderation processes to help prevent abuse and protect our community.
