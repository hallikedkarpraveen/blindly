About Blindly

Blindly is an Indian dating app that replaces swipe culture with blind audio conversations. Built for people exhausted by photo judgments, ghosting, and superficial profiles, Blindly lets users match by current mood and exchange 30-second voice notes. Both people remain anonymous until they mutually consent to reveal each other's photos.

Legal Entity: Ayrav Technologies Private Limited
HQ: India
Platforms: iOS & Android (Launching soon)
Core Safety: Liveness verification, anti-screenshot tracing, zero tolerance child safety
