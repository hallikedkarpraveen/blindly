Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Support Questions

How do I delete my Blindly account and personal data?

+

You have full control over your data. Inside the Blindly app, go to Settings → Account → Delete Account to permanently delete your profile, voice notes, photos, and chat history. You can also request immediate account or waitlist data deletion by emailing info@blindly.date from your registered phone number or email address.

How do I report or block someone on Blindly?

+

Tap the ⋯ (More) or Report icon on any voice prompt, blind profile, or chat thread to block or report a user immediately. Reported accounts are reviewed by our Trust & Safety team. You can also report an incident by emailing safety@blindly.date or visiting our Safety Center.

I joined the waitlist — when will I get access?

+

We are rolling out early access invites in batches across India on iOS and Android. If you have a question about your waitlist spot or want to update your phone number/email, message us on WhatsApp at +91 7795684854 or email info@blindly.date.

How do I contact the Grievance Officer in India?

+

In accordance with the IT Rules, 2021 and DPDP Act, you can reach the Grievance Officer at Ayrav Technologies Private Limited via email at grievance@blindly.date or telephone at +91 7795684854. All grievances are acknowledged within 24 hours and resolved within 15 days.
