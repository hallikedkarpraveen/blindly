Blind datingexplained

Everything you need to know before you go blind.

What is Blindly?

+

Blindly is a blind dating app in India. There are no profile photos or bios. You pick your mood, talk with people who feel the same, and both of you stay anonymous until you agree to reveal.

How does blind dating work on Blindly?

+

Choose your mood, record a 30-second voice intro, and talk with people in the same mood through voice notes and chat. When the vibe feels right, you can each choose to reveal yourself.

When do we see each other’s photos?

+

Only when you both choose to reveal. One yes isn’t enough, so nobody sees your face unless you want them to.

Is Blindly safe?

+

Profiles are verified with a liveness check to stop fake accounts, and your identity stays hidden until a mutual reveal. Screen recording in the app is detected, leaked videos can be traced back to the account they came from, and you can report anyone from their profile or chat. Read more on our safety page.

Is Blindly free?

+

Yes, Blindly is free. You can earn passes by inviting your friends.

Is Blindly on iOS and Android?

+

Blindly is launching on the App Store and Google Play. Join the waitlist to get early access.

Who can use Blindly?

+

Blindly is for adults aged 18 and over. Accounts belonging to anyone under 18 are removed.
